### plotext (piccolomo/plotext)
Bar default marker is `'full'`. `plotext/_kernel/cpp/utility/maps/marker.cpp` L4:
`{"full", L'█'}` = U+2588. Also `brick`=`▇` U+2587, `square`=`■` U+25A0, `emptysquare`=`□` U+25A1,
`circle`=`●` U+25CF, `diamond`=`◆` U+25C6.
**Naming trap:** `eighth`, `beamed`, `flat`, `sharp` are NOT block elements — they map to
`♪ ♫ ♭ ♯` (U+266A U+266B U+266D U+266F), marker.cpp L45-48.
Marker resolution codes (`plotext/_primitives/marker.py` L33-35, `clink.py` L110-111):
`hd` (2x2), `fhd` (2x3), `braille` (2x4). These are **marker/point** kinds, NOT bar fill styles.
`plotext/_kernel/cpp/utility/maps/high_def.cpp` L5-22 hd_lookup 16 entries
(`▗▖▄▝▐▞▟▘▚▌▙▀▜▛█`), L110+ braille_lookup 256 entries, L32 FHD table.
Bar geometry: `docs/source/bar.rst` L55 — `width` default **0.8** = fraction of smallest bar
spacing, `orientation` vertical(default)/horizontal, `lines` and `fill` both drawn.

### asciichart (kroitor/asciichart) — PREMISE CORRECTION
The brief says it uses `█`/`▀`/`▄` and `-` for negatives. **All three are wrong.**
`asciichart.js` L53: `defaultSymbols = ['┼','┤','╶','╴','─','╰','╭','╮','╯','│']`
= U+253C U+2524 U+2576 U+2574 U+2500 U+2570 U+256D U+256E U+256F U+2502 — pure box-drawing,
zero block elements. L98: flat segment = `symbols[4]` `─`. L99-100: corner glyphs `╰╭╮╯`.
L103: vertical fill `│`. There is no negative-bar `-`; negatives simply shift the axis row
(`result[y-min2][offset-1]`), lines are drawn both up and down from the zero row.
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kroitor/asciichart/master/asciichart.js (200)

### gnuplot
`dumb` is ASCII-first: fill char defaults to `"#"` U+0023 (`term/dumb.trm` L1069/1109).
Doc: `fillchar solid` is short for `fillchar "\U+2588"` (FULL BLOCK).
Source: https://gnuplot.sourceforge.net/docs/loc20689.html (read 2026-10-03)
`set term block` adds Unicode: `dot | half | quadrants | sextants | octants | braille | sextpua | octpua`.
Doc notes sextants are Unicode-13-only and font support is still limited.
Source: https://gnuplot.sourceforge.net/docs/loc19798.html (read 2026-10-03)

### termplot (iswdp/termplot), `termplot/term_plot.py` L35
`def plot(x, plot_height=10, plot_char=u'\u25cf')` — default **single repeated glyph** `●` U+25CF.
L5-13 `make_col` fills a column with that one char or space. Caller can pass any char.
L15-23 `make_neg_col` puts negatives **below** the axis.
L25-33 `scale_data` divides by `max(abs(x))` and truncates via `int()`.
L37-38 docstring admits: "This function will make an inaccurate plot if the length of data list
is larger than the number of columns in the terminal."
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/iswdp/termplot/master/termplot/term_plot.py (200)
